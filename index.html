Terms and Conditions

Please read these terms before placing an order.

1. No returns for change of mind

All sales are final for change-of-mind requests. Goods cannot be returned or exchanged simply because a customer changes their mind after delivery or collection.

2. Damaged, spoiled, defective or incorrect goods

If you receive goods that are damaged, spoiled, defective or different from what you ordered, contact us promptly on WhatsApp at 0794 725 287. We will review the complaint and handle it in accordance with applicable consumer protection laws. Nothing in this policy removes rights that cannot legally be excluded.

3. Product prices and availability

Prices and availability are subject to confirmation. Products marked “Price on request” will be priced and confirmed with you before payment. Please do not pay until we confirm your final order total.

4. Delivery fees

Delivery fees are Malaa and environs: KSh 100; Kware and environs: KSh 150; Joska and environs: KSh 170. Store collection has no delivery fee. Please provide accurate delivery details.

5. Payments

Payments are made through M-Pesa Pochi la Biashara at 0794 725 287, after the order and final amount have been confirmed. Keep your M-Pesa transaction confirmation.

6. Order confirmation

Submitting an order request on WhatsApp does not by itself guarantee product availability. We will confirm the order details with you.

7. Contact and complaints

For questions, order changes or complaints, contact Blessed Hands Grocery on WhatsApp at 0794 725 287.
